Hi! I'm Erik 👋🏼

I build free and open-source software for fun and the betterment of mankind 🌎🌍🌏. Founder of Superuser Labs. Building gptme, an open-source AI assistant that turns every developer into a superuser. Founder and maintainer of ActivityWatch, the world's best free and open-source automated time tracker. Previously founding team at Lovable (fka GPT Engineer).

💼 I take on a small number of consulting engagements, see Consulting.
