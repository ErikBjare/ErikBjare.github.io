# Consulting

I'm Erik, creator of [ActivityWatch](https://activitywatch.net) (18k+ stars) and [gptme](https://gptme.org) (4k+ stars), founding team at [Lovable](https://lovable.dev), MSc from LTH.

I take on a small number of consulting engagements alongside my open-source work. 💼

## What I can help with

- **AI agents & LLM integration**: integrating LLM agents into your product, agent infrastructure & evals, tool use, autonomous workflows.
- **Developer tooling**: CLIs, editor/IDE integrations, developer experience, CI/CD.
- **Full-stack engineering**: from prototype to production, Python/TypeScript/Rust.
- **ML & data**: data pipelines, analysis, applied machine learning.

## How I work

- **Project engagements**: short-to-medium projects, weeks to a few months.
- **Advisory retainers**: ongoing access for questions, reviews, and direction.
- **On-site stints**: occasional weeks-long on-site visits; otherwise remote-first.

## Terms

Engagements are B2B through **Superuser Labs Lund AB** (org.nr 559388-1773), a VAT-registered Swedish AB. Remote-first, invoiced from Sweden (SEPA). Rates are discussed per engagement.

Past clients include Strawberry Browser, Pale Blue Dot, Ghent University, and Lovable.

## Get in touch

Sound interesting? Email me at [erik@superuserlabs.com](mailto:erik@superuserlabs.com) with a few lines about what you're building. 🚀
