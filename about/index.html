About

I'm a software engineer, open-source founder, and AI enthusiast based in Sweden. I completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science at Lund University and have been building in the open ever since.

Currently: founder of Superuser Labs. Building gptme — an open-source AI assistant for the terminal. Maintaining ActivityWatch — the world's best free and open-source automated time tracker. Previously founding team at Lovable (fka GPT Engineer).
